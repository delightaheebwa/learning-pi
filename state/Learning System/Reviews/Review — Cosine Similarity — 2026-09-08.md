## Question (definitional)
Explain what cosine similarity measures, why it's used instead of the raw dot product for comparing vectors (e.g. word embeddings), and what the value 0 means in this context.

## Answer
cosine similarity measures how aligned two vectors are in terms of direction. Raw dot product gives too much credit to magnitude hence cosine similarity is used. 0 means that the vectors are orthogonal to each other.
