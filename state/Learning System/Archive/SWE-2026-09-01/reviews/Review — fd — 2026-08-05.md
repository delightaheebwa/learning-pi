**Question:** What makes `fd` different from `find` — and when would you stick with plain `find` instead?

**Answer:** fd is faster with simpler syntax for day-to-day use; stick with find in scripts for other people's machines since it's standard everywhere.

**Assessment:** Correct. Nitpick: find isn't a shell built-in — it's a standard POSIX utility present on essentially every Unix-like system, which is exactly what makes it portable.
