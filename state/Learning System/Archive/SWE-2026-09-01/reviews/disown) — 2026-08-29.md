## Question
Both `nohup cmd &` and `disown` keep a bg job alive after terminal close, but apply at different moments. What is the difference — specifically what you pass to each (command vs job-number)?

## Expected
`nohup cmd &` is a wrapper run BEFORE the job exists — you pass it the command. `disown %1` references an already-running job — you pass it the job-number (%1).

## Learner answer
Correct: nohup runs beforehand (pass the command); disown runs after the job started (pass the job-number).
