## What We Covered

> Summary of the session content.

- Reviewed the difference quotient as average rate of change over a finite interval and the derivative as the limit of that quotient.
- Introduced partial differentiation for functions of multiple variables: differentiate with respect to one variable while holding the others constant.
- Built the gradient from the partial derivatives and connected it to the direction of steepest increase.
- Interpreted negative gradients as the direction of steepest decrease, which is why gradient descent moves opposite the gradient.
- Used small algebraic examples to practice computing partial derivatives and gradients.
