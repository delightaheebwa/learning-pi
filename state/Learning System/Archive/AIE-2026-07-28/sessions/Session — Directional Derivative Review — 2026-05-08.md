## What We Covered

> Summary of the session content.

- Reviewed what a directional derivative measures: the rate of change of a scalar function at a point in a chosen direction.
- Corrected the computation rule: take the gradient with respect to the input variables, then dot it with a unit direction vector.
- Reinforced that the gradient is not taken "with respect to" the direction vector.
- Reviewed `J_f(x)r` as the Jacobian applied to a vector, and connected it to the directional derivative when the vector is a unit direction vector.
