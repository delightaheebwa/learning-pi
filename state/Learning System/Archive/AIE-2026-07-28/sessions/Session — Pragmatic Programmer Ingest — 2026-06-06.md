## Concepts Ingested

### Batch 1 (first review: 2026-06-09)
- **Broken Window Theory** — Don't leave messes unfixed; one broken window invites neglect
- **DRY Principle** — Knowledge/intent duplication, not just code duplication; caching as acceptable violation
- **ETC (Easier To Change)** — Good design is easier to change; fundamental design metric
- **Professional Responsibility** — Own mistakes; no excuses, provide options; "I don't know, but I'll find out"

### Batch 2 (first review: 2026-06-10)
- **Good Enough Software** — Know when to stop; involve users; it will never be perfect
- **Knowledge Portfolio** — Knowledge expires; diversify; language/year; books/month; cross-pollination
- **Tracer Bullets** — Thin end-to-end slices; show glimpses of future; different from prototypes
- **Communication for Developers** — Know audience; dialogue not monologue; study great communicators

### Batch 3 (first review: 2026-06-11)
- **Representational Duplication** — External interface DRY violation; two solutions (code gen or flexible + validation)
- **Uniform Access Principle** — Uniform notation for storage vs computation; accessor functions; properties
- **Interdeveloper Duplication** — Team-level duplication; solutions: communication, librarian, centralized utils
- **Maintenance Mindset** — Always in maintenance mode; design for change from day one
