## What We Covered

> Summary of the session content.

- Distinguished the **difference quotient** as the average rate of change over an interval.
- Distinguished the **derivative** as the instantaneous rate of change at a point.
- Repeatedly connected the derivative to the limit of the difference quotient as the interval shrinks.
- Clarified why plugging in the exact zero step size is invalid: the denominator becomes zero, so the quotient is undefined.
- Reinforced the meaning of numerator as change in output and denominator as change in input.
