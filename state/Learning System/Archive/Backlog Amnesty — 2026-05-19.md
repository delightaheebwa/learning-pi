# Backlog Amnesty — 2026-05-19

> Declared as part of the 2026-05-19 Learning System Audit (Critical Action #1).

## Reason

The review queue contained **13 overflow items** and **10 active queue items** — many over 30 days overdue. The queue had become mathematically impossible to clear under the 5-per-session cap, creating a vicious cycle where concepts decay while waiting, fail on eventual review, and generate even more reviews.

This amnesty removes concepts >30 days overdue from the active review rotation. They remain in the Knowledge Base with their current status and can be explicitly resumed when the learner is ready.

## Revival Protocol

To resume any amnestied concept:
- Say "resume [concept name]" or "resume robotics" or "resume calculus foundations"
- Zo will re-add it to the review queue with a fresh 3-day interval
- The concept retains its previous status (does not reset to `not_started`)
