## Question (definitional)
You have a coin. You flip it 10 times and get 7 heads. Explain how MLE and MAP would each estimate the coin's bias, and why they give different answers. What role does the prior play?

## Answer
MAP uses a prior while MLE doesn't (MLE relies on data alone). The prior injects a belief about plausible values, most helpful with scarce data to mitigate overfitting.
