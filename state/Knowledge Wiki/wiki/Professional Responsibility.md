# Professional Responsibility

A set of attitudes and behaviors for taking ownership of your work as a software developer.

## Core principles

- **Own your mistakes**: Don't deflect or hide errors. Acknowledge them.
- **No excuses, provide options**: When things go wrong, don't explain why it's not your fault — explain what you're going to do about it.
- **"I don't know, but I'll find out"**: Never just say "I don't know." Always follow it with a commitment to discover the answer.
- **Be careful about taking responsibility**: Before you say yes, understand what you're signing up for. But once you do — deliver.

## Why it matters

Trust is built through reliability. People trust developers who:
- Admit when they're wrong
- Fix problems instead of explaining them away
- Follow through on commitments
- Are honest about what they don't know (and then go learn it)
