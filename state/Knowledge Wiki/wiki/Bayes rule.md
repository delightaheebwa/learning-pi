# Bayes rule

Bayes' rule updates a belief after seeing data.

## Core statement

The posterior probability of a hypothesis given data is proportional to:

- the likelihood of the data under that hypothesis
- times the prior probability of the hypothesis

In symbols:

- \(P(H \mid D) \propto P(D \mid H) P(H)\)

## What the parts mean

- **Prior**: what you believed before seeing the data
- **Likelihood**: how well the data fit the hypothesis
- **Posterior**: what you believe after seeing the data

## Why it matters

Bayes' rule is the basic update rule for probabilistic inference. It turns observed evidence into revised beliefs.
