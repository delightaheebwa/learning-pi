# Prior Probability P(H)

> **Source:** Phase 1, Lesson 07 — Bayes' Theorem & Statistical Thinking (AIEFS Rohit)
> **Related:** [[Bayes' Theorem]], [[Posterior Probability]], [[Likelihood]], [[Base Rate Fallacy]]

## Definition

Your belief before seeing any evidence — the base rate. In the Bayes' formula, this is P(H).

## Example

- Disease prevalence: P(disease) = 0.001 (1 in 1000)
- Librarian vs farmer: what fraction of the population are librarians?

## Key Insight

The prior dominates when a condition is rare. Even accurate tests produce mostly false positives when the base rate is low (see [[Base Rate Fallacy]]).
