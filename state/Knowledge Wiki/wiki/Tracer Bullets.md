# Tracer Bullets

A metaphor from The Pragmatic Programmer: in night combat, tracer rounds leave a visible trail so you can see where your shots are landing and adjust. In software, tracer bullets are thin, end-to-end slices of functionality that give visibility into the final system.

## Core ideas

- **Show a glimpse of the future**: People find it easier to get on board with an ongoing success. A working slice — even minimal — is more convincing than a perfect plan.
- **Work out what you can reasonably deliver, then deliver it well**: Don't over-promise. Deliver something real and excellent within a narrow scope, and let users say "it would be nice if..."
- **Build momentum**: A working end-to-end prototype rallies support, clarifies requirements, and exposes problems early.

## Tracer bullets vs Prototypes

Tracer bullets are meant to be kept and built upon — they're the skeleton of the real system. Prototypes are exploratory throwaway code meant to answer questions, then be discarded. Don't confuse the two.
