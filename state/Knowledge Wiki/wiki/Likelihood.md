# Likelihood P(E|H)

> **Source:** Phase 1, Lesson 07 — Bayes' Theorem & Statistical Thinking (AIEFS Rohit)
> **Related:** [[Bayes' Theorem]], [[Prior Probability]], [[Posterior Probability]]

## Definition

How probable the evidence is if the hypothesis is true — restricted to the hypothesis class.

## In the Disease Example

- P(positive|disease) = 0.99 — the likelihood (test catches it)
- P(positive|healthy) = 0.01 — evidence under negated hypothesis (false positive rate)

## Key Distinction

Likelihood is NOT P(H|E). It's P(E|H) — the probability of the data given the hypothesis, not the other way around.
