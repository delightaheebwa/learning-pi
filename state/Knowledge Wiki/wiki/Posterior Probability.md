# Posterior Probability P(H|E)

> **Source:** Phase 1, Lesson 07 — Bayes' Theorem & Statistical Thinking (AIEFS Rohit)
> **Related:** [[Bayes' Theorem]], [[Prior Probability]], [[Likelihood]]

## Definition

Your updated belief after incorporating evidence. This is the output of Bayes' Theorem.

## Formula

$$P(H|E) = \frac{P(E|H) \cdot P(H)}{P(E)}$$

## Disease Example Result

P(disease|positive) ≈ 9% — despite a 99% accurate test, the posterior is only 9% because the prior (base rate) is so low.
