# Knowledge Portfolio

Your knowledge and experience is an **expiring asset**. Like a financial portfolio, it needs active management and diversification.

## Core principles

- **Don't put all your technical eggs in one basket**: Diversify across different technologies so you're better equipped for change.
- **Learn an emerging technology before it becomes popular**: Get in early.
- **Learn at least one new language per year**: Different languages solve the same problems in different ways, broadening your thinking.
- **Read a technical book each month**: Systematic, deep learning.
- **Don't forget non-technical books**: You work with people, not just machines.
- **Experiment with different environments**: Spend time in Windows, Linux, different IDEs — each exposes you to different paradigms.
- **Read news on technology different from your own**: Cross-pollination of ideas.
- **Once comfortable with a technology, move on**: The process of learning expands your thinking even if you never use that tech on a project. Ideas cross-pollinate.

## The cross-pollination idea

Even if your project doesn't use the technology you're learning, the concepts, patterns, and ways of thinking transfer. A functional programming mindset improves your OOP code. A Linux terminal workflow changes how you think about tooling everywhere.
