# Communication for Developers

Software development is a social activity. The best code in the world is useless if you can't communicate what it does, why it matters, or how to use it.

## Core principles

- **Know your audience**: Before communicating, ask: "Does this communicate what I want to express to my audience?" Tailor your message.
- **Listen actively**: Ask someone to rephrase and summarize what you just said. If they can't, you weren't clear.
- **Turn meetings into dialogues**: Don't present at people — converse with them. Make it a two-way exchange.
- **Study great communicators**: Identify people you look up to and analyze how they communicate. What makes them effective?
- **Communication is a skill**: Like coding, it can be studied, practiced, and improved.

## Why it matters for developers

- Code reviews are communication
- Documentation is communication
- Stand-ups, planning, retrospectives — all communication
- Explaining technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders
- Writing clear commit messages, PR descriptions, and issue reports
