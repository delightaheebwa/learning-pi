## P1.1 — Feynman/teach-back is mandatory for graduation
- **Problem:** `feynman: none` in practice; reviews skip explain-back; concepts can look `solid`
  without a successful teach-back.
- **Do:** make explain-back a required beat for `concept`/`design` items in the **review** flow (not
  only at lesson end). Reviews must elicit it and pass `feynman_pass`/`feynman_fail` into the close
  envelope; `review-clerk` records it. Keep `memory`/`procedure` exempt.
- **Files:** `.pi/skills/learning-system/SKILL.md` (review flow), `.pi/agents/review-clerk.md`,
  `.pi/prompts/review.md`; `learning-system/scripts/learner_history.py` (already requires feynman for
  concept/design — confirm and test).
- **Verify:** `ops_test.py` case asserting a `concept` entry cannot be `solid` without a Feynman pass.

## P1.2 — Deterministic MCQ grading + a `q_type` enum
- **Problem:** `q_type` is a free string; MCQ grading is done by the LLM even when the answer key is known.
- **Do:** enforce a documented `q_type` enum in `ops.py attempt` (reject unknown with a clear error);
  add a deterministic helper (e.g. `ops.py grade-mcq --key A,B,C --answers A,C,C`) that returns
  per-item pass/fail, and use it in the review flow where the answer key is known so the LLM only
  handles free-recall grading.
- **Files:** `learning-system/scripts/ops.py`, `scripts/ops_test.py`, `.pi/skills/learning-system/SKILL.md`,
  `.pi/agents/review-clerk.md`.
- **Verify:** `ops_test.py` tests for the enum and the grader (mixed correct/incorrect letters).

## P1.3 — Prerequisite edges in live state
- **Problem:** prerequisites are pedagogical only; nothing refuses downstream teaching over a broken
  foundation.
- **Do:** add optional `prereqs: [names]` to `Attempts.json` concept entries; add `ops.py prereqs
  <concept>` and a check used by the teach/review flow: if a direct prereq is `fuzzy` (or has an open
  mistake), the flow must **refuse to advance** and re-derive the prereq first. Keep it advisory
  where data is missing (no prereq evidence = unknown, do not block).
- **Files:** `learning-system/scripts/ops.py`, `scripts/learner_history.py` (surface prereq state),
  `.pi/skills/learning-teach/SKILL.md`, `.pi/skills/learning-system/SKILL.md`.
- **Verify:** `ops_test.py` for `ops.py prereqs` behavior and the "fuzzy prereq blocks" rule.

## P1.4 — Confidence calibration
- **Problem:** confidence is elicited (`sure|hunch|no idea`) but never measured.
- **Do:** ensure the teaching/review flows record `--confidence` on every graded attempt; add
  `ops.py calibration [TRACK]` that reports, for attempts tagged with confidence: % correct when
  `sure` vs `hunch` (overconfidence/underconfidence), and a calibration flag when `sure`-wrong is
  high. Surface it in `ops.py mastery --json`.
- **Files:** `learning-system/scripts/ops.py`, `scripts/ops_test.py`, `.pi/skills/learning-teach/SKILL.md`,
  `.pi/agents/review-clerk.md`.
- **Verify:** `ops_test.py` with a synthetic overconfident history.

## P1.5 — Transfer beyond near-isomorphic
- **Problem:** transfer is a dimension (P0.4) driven by `transfer` q_types, but nothing schedules far
  transfer or requires it for graduation.
- **Do:** define `q_type` values `transfer-near`/`transfer-far`; make the review flow include a
  transfer item for `concept`/`design` concepts on a cadence; require at least one passed delayed
  transfer for `consolidated` (if/when graduation exists) and surface transfer state.
- **Files:** `learning-system/scripts/ops.py` (enum + dimensions), `.pi/skills/learning-system/SKILL.md`,
  `.pi/skills/learning-teach/SKILL.md`.
- **Verify:** `ops_test.py` for the transfer dimension from `transfer-far` attempts.

## P1.6 — Withhold unverified load-bearing claims (stop rendering `⛔ UNVERIFIED` content)
- **Problem:** after the retry cap the gate still renders unverified content with a banner.
- **Do:** for a `claims` turn whose draft contains **hard facts** (`extractHardFacts`), the retry cap
  must **withhold** the content (render only the banner), not deliver it. Non-load-bearing prose may
  still degrade gracefully. This is a deliberate change to the anti-dead-end policy for hard facts.
- **Files:** `.pi/extensions/learning-gate/gate-core/engine.ts`, `primitives.ts` if needed,
  `contracts/learning-core.json` + `CONTRACT.md`, `test/gate_test.mjs`, `test/golden/golden_test.mjs`.
- **Verify:** gate test: retry cap on a hard-fact claims turn yields a withheld banner with no content.
- **Note:** keep `G-retry-cap` semantics for non-hard-fact turns; add a new invariant.

## P1.7 — Learner-authored wiki ("my understanding")
- **Problem:** the Clerk ghost-writes all durable knowledge; the learner authors none of it.
- **Do:** require a `## My understanding` section on concept wiki pages written in the **learner's own
  words** (captured during the session). The Clerk preserves and appends it; the AI may annotate but
  must not overwrite it. Mark it `status=learner-note` in the page provenance when it is the learner's.
- **Files:** `.pi/skills/llm-wiki/SKILL.md`, `.pi/agents/clerk.md`, `Knowledge Wiki/AGENTS.md` (note),
  `.pi/skills/learning-teach/SKILL.md` (capture the learner's words at consolidate).
- **Verify:** doc/behavior; a sample page format in the skill. (No code gate needed.)

## P1.8 — Seeded-error red-team harness
- **Problem:** no measurement of the gate's false-pass rate.
- **Do:** add a self-contained test that injects known-wrong load-bearing content into sandboxed gate
  flows and asserts the gate catches it (fact-check ISSUES path or `CLAIMS_INCOMPLETE`). Keep it
  offline (no real models) and wire it into `learn-check`.
- **Files:** `test/redteam/` (new) or an addition to `test/gate_test.mjs`, `scripts/learn-check`.
- **Verify:** the harness runs and reports caught/total.

## P1.9 — Fading hint budgets
- **Problem:** hint budget is always 2; no fading as independence grows.
- **Do:** the teaching skill reads the concept's `independence`/`stability` dimensions and shrinks the
  guiding-question budget (e.g. 2 → 1 → 0) as they rise; record `--hints` on attempts so the effect is
  measurable.
- **Files:** `.pi/skills/learning-teach/SKILL.md`, `.pi/APPEND_SYSTEM.md`.
- **Verify:** doc/behavior. If a deterministic check is feasible, add it; otherwise document clearly.
