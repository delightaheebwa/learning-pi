## Findings

### [HIGH] Solo flow leaks teaching on three paths — "impossible by construction" is false
- Where: `.pi/extensions/learning-gate/gate-core/engine.ts:561-566` (solo block), `:920-931`
  (retry cap); `.pi/prompts/solo.md:9`
- Evidence (live probes against the real gate):
  1. `[[TURN:none]]` + fresh teaching prose in `[[FLOW:solo]]` **rendered immediately** (no
     receipt, no withhold). The none-evasion guard (`engine.ts:770-795`) only fires when the text
     matches a verified/in-flight draft, so any novel teaching smuggled in a `none` turn passes —
     and `solo.md:9` itself authorizes `none` lines.
  2. A solo `[[TURN:claims]]` re-emitted 3× rendered as `⛔ UNVERIFIED` **with the teaching content
     attached** (`SOLO_NO_TEACHING` in the code list, content delivered anyway). The retry cap does
     not exempt `SOLO_*`, unlike high/medium issues (`G-issues-no-cap`).
  3. A solo `[[TURN:grade]]` with agreeing grade-audit + fact-checked repair tail **rendered**,
     repair prose included. Tag is `grade`, so the solo block never fires; post-answer explanation
     of the tested material is allowed inside the measurement.
- Why it matters: P0.5's headline guarantee ("Teaching and hints are impossible by construction"
  — `solo.md:5`, `learning-system/SKILL.md`, `FIELD-GUIDE.md`) fails open on all three paths. (1)
    and (2) let an uncooperative or sloppy Tutor teach inside the AI-free measurement; (3) is
    fact-checked prose, so epistemically fine, but it contaminates later items in the same check.
- Recommended fix: exempt `SOLO_NO_TEACHING`/`SOLO_NO_AIDS` from the retry-cap dump (withhold
  outright, as `G-hard-fact-withhold` later did for hard facts); extend the solo block to `none`
  turns carrying substantive content and to `grade` turns with a repair/teaching tail (grade-only
  verdicts stay allowed); add gate tests for all three.

### [HIGH] Solo and claim-completeness guards vanish on the judge path
- Where: `.pi/extensions/learning-gate/gate-core/engine.ts:1057-1450` (`applyAssessment` — no
  `solo` branch, no `CLAIMS_INCOMPLETE`); `gate-core/judge/*` (zero mentions of solo)
- Evidence: `grep SOLO gate-core/judge/` → no hits; `grep -n 'SOLO\|CLAIMS_INCOMPLETE' engine.ts`
  → solo/completeness blockers exist only in `legacyMessageEnd` (lines 561, 671, 728). The judge
  package (`buildTurnPackage`, `:1016-1055`) forwards `flow:"solo"` as context, but no rubric text
  tells the model to withhold teaching, and nothing deterministic enforces it. Same for
  `missingHardFacts`: judge-path claims rely solely on the `fact-check.md` instruction to
  self-enumerate.
- Why it matters: with a judge configured, P0.2's and P0.5's deterministic guarantees silently
  degrade to model behavior — the exact failure mode the audit flagged (same-process LLM as the
  backstop). `G-solo-no-teaching`'s 4 tests all run the legacy path, so `learn-check` stays green
  while the judge path is unguarded.
- Recommended fix: enforce the solo block and `missingHardFacts` deterministically in
  `applyAssessment` before consulting the judge (block `claims`/`viz` in solo; `CLAIMS_INCOMPLETE`
  on the bound draft), or document that the judge path is exempt and add judge-path tests.

### [MEDIUM] `missingHardFacts` containment is substring-based — evadable, and blind to small integers
- Where: `.pi/extensions/learning-gate/gate-core/primitives.ts:507-512`
- Evidence (direct probes): `missingHardFacts('The sample size is n=200.', '…n=2000.')` → `[]`
  (a longer number subsumes the real one — `"200" in "2000"`). `extractHardFacts('Entropy is 5
  bits so perplexity is 32.')` → `[]`: 1–2 digit integers — the most common load-bearing numbers
  in lessons (`k=3`, `PPL is 4`, `2 bits`) — are never extracted. Only decimals, `%`, sci-notation,
  3+ digit ints, LaTeX, URLs are.
- Why it matters: the roadmap claims "the generator can no longer curate its own exam." True only
  for the covered subset: a wrong small integer, or a claim phrased with a subsuming longer
  number, passes the completeness gate unverified. Prose mechanisms/dates-as-words were always out
  of scope (left to the verifier), but small numbers read as covered when they are not.
- Recommended fix: compare on token boundaries (not `String.includes`); either extend extraction
  to small integers with an allowlist for list markers/enumerations, or state the small-integer
  blind spot in `AUDIT-ROADMAP.md` P0.2 and `fact-check.md` so the verifier owns it explicitly.

### [MEDIUM] `extractHardFacts` false positives force junk claims (`3,000` → `000`, `$5…$10`, year 1800)
- Where: `.pi/extensions/learning-gate/gate-core/primitives.ts:481-500`
- Evidence (direct probes): `'The corpus has 3,000 sentences.'` → `["000"]` (comma-split fragment
  becomes a mandatory "hard fact"); `'It costs $5 and pays $10 later.'` → `["5 and pays"]`
  (dollar-pair regex spans the sentence); `'In 1800 …'` → `["1800"]` (`YEAR_RE` excludes only
  19xx/20xx).
- Why it matters: each junk fact must be parroted into `claims[]` or the turn withholds as
  `CLAIMS_INCOMPLETE` — friction/false withholds, and it trains the Tutor to game the claim list.
  Low frequency in ML lessons (LaTeX `$…$` is the intended use), but real.
- Recommended fix: skip `\d{3,}` fragments adjacent to commas (`3,000`); require `$…$` to look
  like math (or ignore plain-currency pairs); extend `YEAR_RE` to `1[789]\d{2}|20\d{2}`-style
  range or drop the year carve-out in favor of verifier judgment.

### [MEDIUM] Provenance lint accepts malformed/meaningless markers — `--check` can never fail on content
- Where: `learning-system/scripts/wiki_provenance.py:34-53,63-81`
- Evidence (direct probes of `read_status`): `status=bogus` → `"unverified"` (passes `--check`);
  `status=verified | source=legacy | verified-by=—` (no evidence of any check) → `"verified"`;
  `status=learner_note` → `"unverified"` (silent reclassification); marker mid-file accepted
  though docs say "first lines"; `source`/`verified-by`/`date` never validated. `scan()`'s
  `invalid` list is dead code (`read_status` only returns `None` or a valid member), so `--check`
  fails only on *missing* markers, never on bad ones. No unit tests exist for the script.
- Why it matters: the trust upgrade (`unverified` → `verified`) is self-declared text with no
  lint behind it; a page stamped `verified` without a source/verifier/date counts as verified.
  The roadmap's "upgraded to `verified` only when a verifier checks it" is unenforced.
- Recommended fix: return the raw status and fail `--check` on unknown values; require
  `source=`, `verified-by=`, `date=` (valid date) for `status=verified`; restrict the marker to
  the first ~5 lines; add a few unit tests (there are none — `ls scripts/` shows only
  `ops_test.py`).

### [MEDIUM] Wiki trust inversion is labeling, not enforcement — contamination path (F2) still open
- Where: `learning-system/AGENTS.md:228-248`, `Knowledge Wiki/AGENTS.md:25-50`, both
  `llm-wiki/SKILL.md` provenance sections
- Evidence: all 220 pages stamped `unverified` (verified by `--check` run: 220 unverified / 0
  missing). But nothing in any flow gates on the marker: Scout still ingests wiki pages as
  context, step-0 personalization still reads them as prior, and the Tutor may still teach an
  `unverified` page's claims as fact — the only barrier is advisory skill text. The P0.6 goal
  ("stop contamination compounding") is achieved as visibility, not as a mechanism.
- Why it matters: honest labeling is real progress, but the roadmap overclaims the effect; the
  audit's F2 loop (synthesis → prior context → re-taught → re-enshrined) runs unchanged, now
  with markers nobody is required to read. Flagging so REVIEW scopes the follow-up (e.g. require
  `verified`-or-source citation for load-bearing claims, or surface the marker in Scout digests).
- Recommended fix: at minimum, require Scout digests to carry each cited page's provenance status
  and require the Tutor to re-ground `unverified` claims in `raw/sources` before teaching them;
  soften the roadmap goal line to "label contamination" until a mechanism lands.

### [MEDIUM] P0.1's State rung reuses the unguarded `none` channel; "just tell me" can still bypass all generation
- Where: `.pi/skills/learning-teach/SKILL.md:235-239`, `.pi/APPEND_SYSTEM.md` (State rung);
  `engine.ts:770-795` (none guard)
- Evidence: the procedural State prompt is a sanctioned `[[TURN:none]]`, and whether a prompt
  "embeds any substantive framing" (→ must be `claims` + fact-checked) is judged by the Tutor LLM
  with no deterministic check — a framing-laden "prompt" tagged `none` renders unverified (same
  hole as the solo `none` finding; here it is a P0.1-created sanctioned use of that channel).
  Separately, the "mini still closes with one minimal generation" is declinable: decline → logged
  as `declined_generation` → zero learner generation for the mini. So "just tell me" + one
  decline bypasses all generation; the dependency log measures but never compensates (no
  required re-probe, no block). P0.1 has zero automated tests ("No gate code changed… Consistency
  grep clean") — not even a `learn-check` grep asserting the `dependency_events[]` plumbing.
- Why it matters: the audit's F5 (answer-dependence) is instrumented, not reduced; the roadmap's
  "honored but recorded and compensated" promises compensation that does not exist beyond a
  declinable question.
- Recommended fix: acknowledge in the roadmap that opt-out remains total (measurement only); add
  a cheap `check_learning_layer.py`/grep assertion that `dependency_events[]` survives end to end
  (Pending Ingest → clerk → session note/Learning Record); consider a re-probe rule after N
  consecutive `just_tell_me` events (P1/P2 scope).

### [MEDIUM] P0.4 heuristics defensible but `stability` contradicts "never a false 0"; `--mode`/`--confidence` unvalidated
- Where: `learning-system/scripts/ops.py` (`compute_dimensions`, `independence_ok`,
  `do_attempt` arg parsing), `scripts/learner_history.py` (`tag()`)
- Evidence: `stability = min(3, interval_index)` is always an int (new concept → 0) while the
  docstring promises "A dimension with no evidencing attempt is `None` (unknown), never 0."
  `--mode`/`--confidence` accept any string (`ops.py attempt … --mode Solo` silently records a
  non-`"solo"` mode that `independence_ok` ignores — case-sensitive `== "solo"`). A stale failed
  solo blocks `solid` until another solo is taken (normal passes never clear it) — intended veto
  per the roadmap ("neutral for a failed solo behind later passes"), but a bad-question solo fail
  has no appeal path except retake (no `amend` for `mode` in P0 scope — P2.5 later adds amendable
  `mode`, out of scope here).
- Why it matters: minor individually; collectively the dimension layer is a heuristic with sharp
  edges (a typo'd `--mode` silently voids the independence signal the whole P0.4/P0.5 chain rests
  on). Backward compatibility itself is clean (fields recorded only when supplied; existing
  callers unchanged; grandfathering honest).
- Recommended fix: validate `--mode ∈ {normal,solo}` (case-insensitive) and `--confidence`
  values at the CLI (exit 2 like `--hints`); make `stability` `None` when the entry has no
  attempts, or fix the docstring; note the no-appeal veto in the skill.

### [LOW] Tail bound (`extraTokenCount ≤ 12`) is empirically gentle, but untested on the allow side
- Where: `primitives.ts:441-464`, `test/gate_test.mjs` (P0.2 tail test)
- Evidence (probes): reordered/punctuated re-emission ≈ 0 extra; short framing ("Here is the
  idea in short" + draft) = 6 extra → allowed; the withheld test tail was 20+ extra. A 13+
  token legit addition (e.g. appending a source citation URL, which tokenizes to many tokens)
  would withhold as `FACT_CHECK_MISMATCH` → retry loop → at cap, hard-fact turns are now withheld
  outright (P1.6, out of P0 scope) — i.e. over-strict framing can convert a good turn into a
  withhold rather than a re-verification prompt.
- Why it matters: P0.2's "reordering/punctuation tolerated" is covered by exactly one test on
  the block side; no test pins the allow side (reordered draft renders). A future threshold tweak
  has no guardrail.
- Recommended fix: add one allow-side assertion (reordered/punctuated re-emission renders); note
  in the roadmap that appended citations must be part of the verified draft, not post-hoc.

## Verdict

P0 does roughly what `AUDIT-ROADMAP.md` claims, with honest deviations recorded (fuzzy-not-hash
binding, ledger-under-home-not-state, grandfathered independence) and all four new/changed
invariants covered by passing tests (`learn-check` OK, `ops_test` 48 OK, `wiki_provenance --check`
exit 0). The ledger (P0.3), dimensions/independence scaffolding (P0.4), and wiki labeling (P0.6)
are solid additive work with only secondary gaps (silent ledger drops, unvalidated `--mode`,
self-declared `verified`). P0.2's hard-fact completeness is a genuine improvement with defined
edges (small integers, substring containment, junk-fact FPs) that should be documented, not
shipped silently. The single most important thing to fix is the **solo-flow leakage (finding 1,
compounded by finding 2 on the judge path)**: `/solo` is advertised as "impossible by
construction" while teaching enters freely via `none` turns, retry-cap dumps, and (on the judge
path) all turn types. Exempt `SOLO_*` from the retry cap, block substantive `none`/repair-tail
content in solo, and enforce both deterministically on the judge path — otherwise the
independence signal P0.4/P0.5 exists to produce is untrustworthy.
